However, this relationship broke down across active neuronal subtypes, where neither E-P proximity nor chromatin accessibility predicted transcriptional output. Thus, 3D genome organization distinguishes transcriptionally competent from inactive neuronal states without quantitatively specifying transcriptional output. Our findings support a model in which E-P proximity establishes a permissive structural state, while additional cell-type-specific regulatory mechanisms tune transcriptional output.
