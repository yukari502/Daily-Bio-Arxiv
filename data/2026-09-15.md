Aligning cell representations with text embeddings from the bi-level knowledge databases, enabling knowledge transfer from textual spaces to cellular spaces and effectively mitigating the heterogeneous gap ($>$LLM-based scFMs). Through extensive experiments on six downstream single-cell analysis tasks, we demonstrate that RAGCell achieves outstanding performance compared to state-of-the-art scFMs while operating at less than $\sim$1/10 the cost of pretrain-based scFMs.
