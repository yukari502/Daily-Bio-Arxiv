Main category: q-bio.MN

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: The authors inverse-design thermodynamically consistent CRNs and use implicit differentiation to train the free-energy landscape directly—standard chemical potentials, transition-state energies, and thermodynamic drives. Networks are built from elementary ligation and cleavage steps.

Result: Larger networks fit increasingly complex nonmonotonic polynomial functions, with expressivity scaling logarithmically with network size and predicted primarily by the number of reactions. Abstract: Living systems compute with chemistry by mapping environmental signals onto specific internal chemical states.
