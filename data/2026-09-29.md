*Isaac Ellmen,David Errington,Matthew I. J. Raybould,Charlotte M. Deane*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: Chaining Dropout then LayerNorm (common in AlphaFold2-based predictors) creates a systematic evaluation-time bias. The authors derive a closed-form first-order correction (DLC) that matches MC dropout ensembles and improves accuracy across 10 models with negligible cost.

Abstract: Although $\mathbb{E}[\mathrm{dropout}(x)] = x$, here we show that $\mathbb{E}[\mathrm{LayerNorm}(\mathrm{dropout}(x))]$ is not equal to $\mathrm{LayerNorm}(x)$.
