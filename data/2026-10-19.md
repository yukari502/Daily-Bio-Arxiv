That attractive force determines the typical dwell time of the closed configuration where the gene is most active, and strongly depends on the ligand bulk concentration, contributing to the sensitivity of gene expression. These results suggest a general mechanism and theoretical framework for the precise regulation of genes through tunable genome organization.
